# IKD prototype acceptance

On 27 September 2026, the user identified themself as the clinical reviewer,
tested the local Atlas assessment, reported that it worked as expected, and
offered to sign off. They asked that this stage prioritize the complete
application flow: run rule sets, produce a total per-rule score, and hand over
possible diagnoses. Clinical accuracy and calibration will be worked on after
the application is complete.

This is acceptance of the questionnaire and workflow **for the proof of concept**.
It does not approve the illustrative two-feature threshold as a diagnostic
criterion, establish sensitivity or specificity, or authorize clinical use.
The detailed decisions in `review-record.json` remain pending for future
clinical validation. One reviewer is sufficient for this prototype.
